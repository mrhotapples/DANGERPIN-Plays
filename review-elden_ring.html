Elden Ring (2022)

  • Developer: FromSoftware
  • Platform: PC
  • Genre: Action RPG
  • Release date: February 25, 2022
  • Played for: 843 hours
9.5/10

Overview

Elden Ring is the magnum opus from visionary game designer Hidetaka Miyazaki and his crew at FromSoftware. To say that this game saved my life would be an understatement. When things are out of control, a safe challenge can help you get a grip. Elden Ring allows you to safely fail, over and over again, until you succeed. Or give up...

Gameplay

Elden Ring refines the core gameplay formulas introduced in Demon's Souls (2009) and brought to maturity in the interconnected world of Dark Souls (2011). Combat is the primary focus of the game, and it is as rewarding as it is challenging. Players may select from a handful of starter classes or customize their own Tarnished, allowing free expression of whatever playstyle best suits them. Along the way, weapons may be upgraded, imbued with affinities like fire or bleed, and mixed-and-matched in countless ways. Armor lends protection, but cosmetics are equally important (we call it "Fashion Souls") and there are dozens of armor sets to choose from.

Story

The story of Elden Ring is as convoluted and epic as it is simple and human. Many people play this game without realizing there is a story at all, but if you go looking it's there, and you will never fully understand it. The basics are this: Marika The Eternal, is the vessel for the titular Elden Ring, granting her Godlike powers over life, death, and reality itself. She reformed the world to match her will, but becoming God did not nullify Marika's humanity. She goes mad and shatters the Elden Ring to bits, sending fragments of its runic power across the farthest reaches of The Lands Between. The player is called to claim the Elden Ring and repair what Marika has broken, to bring about the dawning of a new age as Elden Lord.

Thankfully, you'll have your trusty spirit horse Torrent by your side. A host of interesting characters will appear throughout the worlds half a dozen or so regions, some with plans of their own...plans that may or may not align with yours. They each have their own stories themselves, and the game offers several different endings to reflect the choices of which factions you ultimately decide to consort with. There are deathbed companions, Golden Order fundamentalists, and sorcerors waiting to meet you. But do you need to pay attention to any of this to enjoy the game? No! So don't get too hung up on it, but if you want a rabbit hole...this is it.

Graphics and Sound

Elden Ring's visuals are nothing to scoff at. The art design is incredible and every moment playing this game is a visual spectacle unlike any other. It really is that profound. Many times, I found myself riding along on Torrent in wonderment at the vistas, the cliffs, the architecture... The detail put into meticulously constructing The Lands Between is staggering to comprehend. Movement is meaningful in elden Ring, and the exaggerated animations of most characters make it easy to spot their tells; you start to quickly remember how long each wind-up is, how quickly they move, or which attacks are linked together. In this way, the visual deisgn is actually core to the gameplay experience; it isn't something added on top. The single blemish on the game's graphical presentation is the engine it runs on. To be succinct, it's just not optimized well. On my RTX 5070 + Ryzen 5700X setup, there are still frame pacing issues and the game struggles to maintain 60fps in some scenes.

The sound design, to its credit, is practically flawless. Voice acting is top-notch and these are among the best performances I've ever heard, video game or otherwise. The music is the perfect backdrop for your explorations, battles, and even those occasional moments of respite. Effects are impactful, rich, and meaningfully connected to what's happening on screen. When your character successfully executes a parry, you'll know it by the sound it makes. When you kill an enemy, not only do they combust into runic dust and woft away with the breeze, but you also hear that signature "schink"...the same sound that plays when you yourself take a fatal blow. Then the gong plays, your Tarnished falls to their knees, and you are (perhaps mockingly) reminded of what's happened: You Died. It all goes to say that the sound in Elden Ring enhances the visual storytelling in the same way the graphical presentation enhances the gameplay. It's all one big, beatufiul package.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Peak Soulslike combat
  • Amazing art design
  • Incredible world

Cons

  • Punishing difficulty
  • Engine quirks and performance problems
  • Story is hard to fully grasp

Final Verdict

If you can grow into it, Elden Ring will change your life. If you need your hand held, or you require a lot of positive reinforcement to keep playing, this isn't the right game for you. Stat upgrades matter less than your actual skill at the game, and the true progression comes from improving yourself, not the gear or your character level.
